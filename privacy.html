Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

1. Overview

This privacy policy describes how Movia Nail Content Publisher handles information when used to prepare and publish social media content for Movia Nail.

2. Information and Content

The tool may process product images, captions, and related content provided by the operator.

If TikTok authorization is used, the tool may process authorization credentials and information returned by TikTok APIs to perform the requested publishing actions.

3. Storage and Security

Information is handled according to the actual configuration of the tool. Authorization credentials must be kept confidential and protected against unauthorized access.

4. Sharing and Third Parties

Content submitted for publishing is transmitted to TikTok when the operator authorizes the publishing action. TikTok's own privacy policy and terms apply to its services.

We do not sell personal information.

5. Retention and Deletion

Information should be retained only as long as necessary for the tool's operation. Locally stored files and credentials should be deleted when no longer needed.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the tool or its data handling practices.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, contact Movia Nail through the official store website.
